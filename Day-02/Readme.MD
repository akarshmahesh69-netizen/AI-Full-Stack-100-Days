# Day 2 — Software Thinking, Business Problems & MVP

## What I Learned

* Business problem → software solution
* User vs stakeholder
* Requirements thinking
* MVP thinking
* Product and data thinking
* HTML structure vs database data
* Basic testing and debugging

## Business Problem

Used a local B2B/B2C clothing-store example to understand how a real business problem can be translated into a website solution.

## User and Stakeholder

* User: Customer / potential customer
* Stakeholder: Business owner

## Requirements Practiced

The website should allow customers to:

* View business information
* View working days and opening hours
* Browse products
* See product prices
* See available sizes
* See product availability
* Contact the store through Call / WhatsApp

## MVP Thinking

Learned to identify the smallest useful version of a product before adding advanced features.

For the clothing-store example, the MVP focused on:

* Business information and timings
* Products with price, size and availability
* Clear contact actions

Future ideas such as AI assistance, WhatsApp AI and automation were treated as later-stage features rather than core MVP requirements.

## What I Built

Improved a simple clothing-store webpage by adding:

* Product information
* Product details
* Trending Products section
* Product prices
* Available sizes
* Product availability
* Three products: T-Shirt, Jeans and Hoodie
* Product enquiry button

## What I Practiced

* Added and modified HTML
* Structured product information using lists
* Tested changes in the browser
* Practiced identifying a possible HTML/CSS issue
* Thought about how product data could be stored separately from HTML
* Understood why a database can store changing product information

## Key Learning

HTML provides the structure and content displayed by the webpage.

A database can store changing business information such as:

* Products
* Prices
* Sizes
* Availability
* Business information

The website can read the data and display the appropriate information.

## Testing

Checked that:

* All three products appeared
* Price, sizes and availability were displayed
* The enquiry button appeared
* The webpage continued to display correctly

## What I Demonstrated

* Identified users and stakeholders
* Converted a business problem into requirements
* Grouped requirements into an MVP
* Explained HTML vs database
* Explained why changing product data should not require changing page structure
