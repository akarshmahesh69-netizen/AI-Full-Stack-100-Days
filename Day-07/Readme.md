## Learning

Today I practiced using GitHub Copilot as an AI coding assistant.

Copilot initially suggested retail-oriented services such as tailoring, styling, and alterations. These did not match my business because my business is a B2B manufacturer and wholesaler.

I provided additional business context and asked Copilot to suggest services relevant to B2B buyers.

The second suggestion was more relevant and included:

- Custom Fabric & Material Development
- Bulk Manufacturing & MOQ Support
- Fast Production & Delivery Scheduling

## Practical Work
